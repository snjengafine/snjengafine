## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🏥 HealthVault — Healthcare Records & Hospital Management Platform

**HealthVault** is a healthcare information platform designed to improve how patient information is managed and shared across healthcare organizations.

The project explores how modern software architecture can be applied to healthcare workflows while maintaining **structured data, authorization, auditability, and secure access to patient records**.

**Core areas include:**

* 🏥 Hospital & facility management
* 👤 Patient management
* 📋 Electronic medical records
* 🩺 Clinical encounters & diagnoses
* 💊 Medications & prescriptions
* 🧪 Laboratory orders & results
* ❤️ Vital signs
* 👨‍⚕️ Healthcare staff & role management
* 🔐 Authentication & authorization
* 📜 Audit trails
* ⛓️ Blockchain-related healthcare data concepts
* 📊 Analytics & reporting

**Architecture:**

`Angular Frontend → REST API → Services → Repositories → PostgreSQL`

**Backend:**

`Python → FastAPI → SQLAlchemy → PostgreSQL → Alembic`

**Frontend:**

`Angular → TypeScript → Angular Material → SCSS`

🔗 **[View HealthVault on GitHub](https://github.com/snjengafine/HealthVault-Enterprise)**

### 🚚 LETland — Business Management System

**LETland** is a full-stack business management system designed to bring core business operations into one platform.

The system focuses on managing business activities such as inventory, sales, transport, purchasing, customers, employees, and operational workflows.

**Core areas include:**

* 📦 Inventory & product management
* 💰 Sales & payments
* 🚚 Deliveries & transport
* 🚛 Trucks & trips
* 👥 Customers & employees
* 🏭 Suppliers & purchases
* 📋 LPOs
* 📊 Business operations
* 🗄️ Business data management

**Architecture:**

`Frontend → API → Services → Repositories → SQLite`

**Focus:**

`Business Processes → Data → APIs → Application Logic → User Interface`

🔗 **[View LETland on GitHub](https://github.com/snjengafine/Letland)**

## 🏗️ How I Build

I believe good software starts with understanding the **problem before writing the code**.

My development process is centered around:

```text
Understand the Problem
        ↓
Design the Data
        ↓
Design the Architecture
        ↓
Build the Backend
        ↓
Build the Frontend
        ↓
Test
        ↓
Debug
        ↓
Improve
```

The goal isn't simply to make software that works.
